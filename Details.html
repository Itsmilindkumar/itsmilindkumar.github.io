Marketing Automation & CRM Build

Live Inbound Pipeline

Most of my outreach was going through the same manual bottleneck: someone shows interest, I have to notice, then I have to sort who's actually worth following up with. This project was about removing that bottleneck entirely.

Built end to end in HubSpot: a landing page with a single clear CTA, leading into a lead-capture form. The moment someone submits, two things happen automatically, they get an email with my resume, and the CRM classifies them into MQL, SQL or Hot based on how they engaged. No manual triage, no lead sitting unsorted in an inbox.

It's live right now: 246844329.hs-sites-na2.com/home.

Growth Automation

AI-Automated Lead Funnel

I didn't want to build a full automated outreach system on a guess. So the sequence mattered: prove the idea first, cheaply, before committing to the build.

Step one was a simple landing page tested with targeted Meta Ads and Google Ads campaigns, just to see whether the concept converted at all before spending time on infrastructure. Once it did, 3.42% CTR and €0.50 per lead, I built the actual system: a Framer landing page feeding into Make.com, which handles the workflow logic, with Gemini generating the personalised copy for each outreach email.

The result is a lightweight, no-code automation stack that finds and reaches people without me writing each message by hand.

Market Research & Growth Strategist, tapeservice

Market expansion for a B2B manufacturer

The brief was broad on purpose: which of 5 European markets, across 3 high-volume sectors, was actually worth expanding into. That meant the research had to be wide before it could get narrow.

I sourced a target company list and automated personalised survey outreach through Power Automate, which generated 30+ responses, then supplemented that with direct stakeholder interviews to get at what the numbers alone couldn't: actual buying behaviour and demand-side reasoning. From there, I layered in segmentation and market-dynamics analysis to see where the real opportunities were.

The output was a market-entry framework and KPI model that ranked every market-sector combination by strategic fit, size and competitive landscape, visualised in Power BI dashboards so senior stakeholders could see the reasoning, not just the recommendation.

Cognizant Technology Solutions

An end-to-end leave-management MVP

Leave requests were going through a manual chain: employee asks, manager approves, someone updates the calendar and dashboard by hand. Every step was a place for something to get lost or delayed.

I automated the full flow so a request routes for approval and updates the calendar and dashboard on its own, removing the manual coordination that used to sit between each step.

Product Demand Validation

Before committing further resources to a product idea, I wanted real signal, not opinion. So I built a custom landing page and ran interest-targeted Meta ad campaigns against it, then used the campaign data itself to keep tightening the funnel.

That iteration is what pushed the result to 3.92% CTR, 4× the industry benchmark, while cutting cost-per-result by 57%.

New Product Development & Marketing

Runner Safety App Concept

The starting point was a real problem: runners, especially solo or off-hours, don't have a good way to signal distress or avoid unsafe density spikes in real time. I designed the concept in Figma around two core features, crowd-density mapping and a built-in SOS.

Rather than build the full product on a hunch, I validated the demand first: user surveys, TAM/SAM/SOM sizing, and a competitor scan to see what was already out there and where the gap actually was.
